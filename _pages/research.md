I am broadly interested in using geospatial data, machine learning, and econometric methods to study a
wide range of environmental and human challenges. Some of the key topics I have worked on or continue to
explore include (hover or tap a tile to read more):

<div class="research-tiles">

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">
    <div class="flip-card-inner">
      <div class="flip-card-front"><h2>Floods</h2></div>
      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>I have built a tool called Global Flood Mapper that maps flood extent using Sentinel-1 SAR imagery
        as soon as it becomes available, and enables impact assessment using land cover and gridded population
        datasets. Users can download the resulting maps in geospatial formats or share an interactive map with
        others for exact reproducibility, all in the cloud without having to download anything or write a
        single line of code.</p>
        <p>Beyond mapping flood extent, I am also deeply interested in assessing the impacts of floods. My
        doctoral dissertation examines the causal impacts of floods on agricultural productivity.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">
    <div class="flip-card-inner">
      <div class="flip-card-front"><h2>Agriculture</h2></div>
      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>As part of my doctoral work, I am trying to generate high-resolution agriculture maps in
        smallholder, data-scarce settings. Lately, I have been exploring the use of foundation models under
        zero-shot and few-shot learning settings to generate reliable agriculture maps.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">
    <div class="flip-card-inner">
      <div class="flip-card-front"><h2>Urban Analysis</h2></div>
      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>Earlier in my research career, I worked extensively on urban spatial analysis, including estimating
        city-scale building heights from satellite imagery, modeling urban growth and land-use change,
        extracting road network hierarchy and connectivity, and studying drivers of urban air pollution. This
        work shaped how I approach spatial problems more broadly, and while my interests have since grown to
        span a wider range of human and environmental challenges, urban systems remain a lens I still bring to
        new research questions.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
