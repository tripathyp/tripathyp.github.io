I am broadly interested in using geospatial data, machine learning, and econometric methods to study a
wide range of environmental and human challenges. Some of the key topics I have worked on or continue to
explore include (hover or tap a tile to read more):

<div class="research-tiles">

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">
    <div class="flip-card-inner">
      <div class="flip-card-front"><h2>Floods</h2></div>
      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>I built Global Flood Mapper, a cloud-based tool that maps flood extent from Sentinel-1 SAR and
        assesses impacts on land cover and population, and I develop deep learning methods to separate
        natural flooding from managed inundation (e.g. irrigation) in SAR imagery.</p>
        <p>I also study how flood risk is perceived versus actually assessed across India, and my
        dissertation examines the causal impacts of floods on agricultural livelihoods.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">
    <div class="flip-card-inner">
      <div class="flip-card-front"><h2>Agriculture</h2></div>
      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>I generate high-resolution agriculture maps in smallholder, data-scarce settings, working on
        field boundary delineation and crop type classification using geospatial foundation models,
        including one of India's first high-resolution crop type maps.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
