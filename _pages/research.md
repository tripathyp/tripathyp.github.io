I build environmental measurements that do not yet exist, using satellite data and
machine learning, and use them to answer cause-and-effect questions about policy. The
tiles below are the areas where I work (hover or tap a tile to read more):

<div class="research-tiles">

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">
    <div class="flip-card-inner">
      <div class="flip-card-front" style="background-image: url('/images/tiles/floods.jpg');"><h2>Floods</h2></div>
      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>I built Global Flood Mapper for SAR-based flood mapping, and use deep learning to separate
        natural flooding from managed inundation. My dissertation studies how flood risk is perceived
        versus actually assessed, and its causal impacts on agricultural livelihoods, in India. This
        work is supported by a NASA FINESST award.</p>

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">

      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>I generate high-resolution agriculture maps in smallholder, data-scarce settings, working on
        field boundary delineation and crop type classification using geospatial foundation models,
        including one of India's first high-resolution crop type maps.</p>
      </div>

  <div class="flip-card" tabindex="0">
    <div class="flip-card-inner">
      <div class="flip-card-front" style="background-image: url('/images/tiles/impact.jpg');"><h2>Impact Evaluation</h2></div>
      <div class="flip-card-back">
        <p>Most policy questions turn on what would have happened otherwise, and in the real world
        that counterfactual is almost never observed. I build machine learning models to generate
        it. One is a physics-aware transformer that predicts the deforestation the Amazon would
        have seen without protection, which is what makes it possible to measure what protected
        areas actually achieved.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

## Funding

Evaluating Uncertainty in Existing Global Flood Exposure Estimates.
- **Goodchild Fellowship in GIS** ($3,400), 2026. Hidden Pitfalls in Geospatial AI.
- **UCSB Department of Geography Summer Research Fellowship** ($8,130), 2024.
- **Schmidt Family Foundation Research Accelerator** ($8,000), 2023. Mapping Smallholder
Agriculture Field Boundaries in the Indo-Gangetic Plain, India.
- **Benioff Applied Environmental Science Summer Fellowship** ($6,000), 2023. Using
Satellites and AI to Map Smallholder Agriculture Field Boundaries With Limited Training Data.
- **GEO – Microsoft Planetary Computer** ($120,000), 2021. India Flood Monitoring and
Impact Assessment Portal, as Co-Investigator.
