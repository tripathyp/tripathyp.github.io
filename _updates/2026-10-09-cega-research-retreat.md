I'll be presenting my work on <em>Physics-constrained deep learning reveals where protected areas
reduced Amazonian deforestation</em> at CEGA's Research Retreat (R²) in Berkeley. The work builds a
multimodal deep learning model that predicts the deforestation a protected area would have
experienced without protection, producing spatially explicit counterfactuals at 1 km resolution
across the Amazon basin for 1996–2020. This lets us estimate the effectiveness of individual
protected areas — and map variation within them — rather than reporting a single average treatment
effect. R² is an annual gathering of researchers across CEGA's campuses working on impact
evaluation, and this year includes a dedicated poster session.
